Give Every Account Its Own Password

A password protects access to your account. If you reuse it, a stolen password from one website could put your other accounts at risk too.

Build Better Password Habits

  1. Use a long, unique password for every account.
  2. Let a password manager generate and store passwords.
  3. Protect your password manager with a strong master password.
  4. Turn on multifactor authentication, or MFA, when available.
  5. Keep recovery codes somewhere safe and private.

What Does MFA Mean?

MFA asks for another way to prove it is you, in addition to your password. This could be an authenticator app or a security key. Never give another person an unexpected verification code.
