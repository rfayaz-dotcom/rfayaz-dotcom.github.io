Start with the Basics

Explore these three topics to build safer online habits.

Password Safety

Use a long, unique password for each account. A password manager can help you create and store your passwords.

Turn on multifactor authentication when available to add another layer of protection.

Recognize Phishing

Be careful with unexpected messages that ask you to click a link, send money, or share personal information.

If a message seems suspicious, contact the organization through its official website or a phone number you already trust.

Protect Your Privacy

Think before sharing your location, birthday, or other personal details online.

Review privacy settings and app permissions so you can choose who sees your information and what your apps can access.

Quick Safety Tips

  • Keep your devices and apps updated.
  • Use a different password for each account.
  • Pause before opening unexpected links or attachments.
  • Never share your passwords or verification codes.
  • Back up important files regularly.
