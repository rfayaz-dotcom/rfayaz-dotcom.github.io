Choose What You Share

Online privacy means making thoughtful choices about your personal information. Small changes to your settings can help you control who sees your posts and what your apps can access.

Review Your Settings

  • Check whether your social media posts are public or limited to people you choose.
  • Review location, camera, microphone, and contact permissions for apps.
  • Remove access for apps you no longer use.
  • Think before sharing your birthday, home address, school schedule, or travel plans.
  • Ask permission before posting someone else's personal information or photo.

A Simple Example

A weather app may need your location to show a local forecast. Review whether it needs your location all the time or only while you use it. You may also be able to enter a city manually.

Private Browsing Has Limits

Private browsing helps limit the history saved in your browser after the private session ends. It does not make you anonymous to websites, your school, your employer, or your internet provider.

Your Privacy Check

  1. Choose one app you use often.
  2. Open its privacy and permission settings.
  3. Turn off access that is unnecessary for the features you use.
  4. Review who can see your profile and posts.
